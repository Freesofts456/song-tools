Song Tools

Free tools for writing and playing songs. They run in your browser, and nothing you type is uploaded.

Chord Shift

Paste a chord sheet, pick a new key, and get a capo suggestion. Works with chords above lyrics or ChordPro brackets.

Open Chord Shift

Swara Sketch

Type Sinhala lyrics and get a playable melody for every line, shown in sargam or Western note names.

Open Swara Sketch
Sample songs in Chord Shift are public domain. The sample lyrics in Swara Sketch were written for this site.
